Welcome to the Customer 360 Help Center. This guide will walk you through the core navigation, interface panels, and premium features of the client intelligence dashboard.

Welcome to Customer 360

Welcome to the Customer 360 Help Center, your comprehensive guide to understanding and navigating the Customer 360 client intelligence dashboard. This guide will take you through the essential features of the platform, including core navigation, key interface panels, customer information, leads, cases, mandates, card details, and other important customer-centric functionalities. It is designed to help you quickly understand how information is organized, where to find the details you need, and how to make the most of the dashboard’s premium features for a complete and efficient view of customer relationships and interactions.

⚡ Customer information

The Customer 360 dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the customer’s personal, contact, address, and profile information, allowing users to quickly access and review essential customer details in one centralized location.

🎨 Banking Relationships

The Customer 360 dashboard provides a consolidated view of the customer’s accounts, financial holdings, active mandates, cases, leads, and related contacts, enabling users to easily access and review important banking relationships, service interactions, and associated customer information from a single centralized interface.

🎨 Customer Insights

The Customer 360 dashboard provides access to security information, rewards, customer activities, compliance details, and contextual insights, enabling users to gain a comprehensive understanding of the customer’s overall profile, engagement, and relationship with the organization.

1. Customer Search

To view customer information, start from the Customer Search Interface. This is the main page where you can search and find customer records using details such as customer ID or other available identifiers.

Figure 1.1: Customer Search portal screen featuring query input and quick navigation triggers.
Figure 1.2: Customer Search portal interface with the search query category dropdown expanded.
1

Launch Search

Open the portal page. Select the required search option from the dropdown, such as Customer ID, Phone Number, Email, or Account Number. Enter the corresponding details in the search box and click Search to find the customer’s information.

2

Select Client Profile

If you search using Phone Number, Email, or Account Number, the matching customer profiles will be displayed below the search bar. Select the required customer from the list to open and view their details.

2. Dashboard Navigation

The Profile Dashboard provides a centralized and comprehensive view of the customer, bringing key identity, relationship, and engagement information together in a single interface. It enables users to quickly understand the customer’s profile, relationship with the organization, and available services, while providing easy access to deeper customer insights and actions.

Figure 2.1: Cinematic Avatar status section sitting at the center of the dashboard workspace.
Figure 2.2: Main Customer 360 Dashboard showing customer profile sidebar and quick-access modules.
Figure 2.3: Dashboard detailed card preview modal displaying structured key-value client metadata.

👤 Large Left Profile Sidebar

Displays detailed contact information, the client's tier classification (e.g., Platinum), branch details, and the assigned Relationship Manager's contact card.

🧭 Sticky Header Bar

Provides theme selectors, alerts toggles, profile editing shortcuts, and the system settings hamburger dropdown (⋮).

🌐 Central Cinematic Workspace

Houses a 3D avatar orb showing visual status. Below the orb sits the quick-access grid containing dynamic modules (Holding, Leads, Cases, Cards, etc.) that open inside the modal overlay.

⚙️ Settings & Help Menu

Click the hamburger button (⋮) in the header to access options, trigger the system tour, or open this Help Center.

3.1. Portfolio Holdings

The **Portfolio Holdings Explorer** is a decoupled module inside the quick-access grid that gives you complete visibility into the client's financial relationships with the bank.

Figure 3.1: Portfolio Holdings panel featuring category tabs, account lists, and auto-spaced detail values.

When you click the **Holding** module, you are presented with three high-level category cards:

  • Assets: Highlights loans, investments, and capital resources.
  • Liabilities: Details checking (CASA) accounts, savings, and term deposits.
  • Value-Added Services: Tracks active mandates, standing instructions, insurance, and benefits.
1

Double-Panel Explorer

Clicking any category launches a double-panel explorer modal. The left side lists all linked bank accounts, and the right side displays fields and balances.

2

Search & Filter

Use the search input at the top of the account list to instantly filter accounts by name or number.

3

Dynamic Canvas Spacing

The right preview panel dynamically checks the pixel width of value strings offscreen using an HTML5 Canvas. If a value overflows the standard column, it automatically expands (spans) to two columns or full width to prevent clipping.

3.2. Leads

The **Leads Module** tracks all active opportunities, prospects, and sales pipelines associated with the customer. It helps relationship managers stay on top of open deals, track interactions, and prioritize follow-ups.

Figure 3.2: Leads tracking pipeline showing open deals, sales status, and follow-up activities.

📈 Sales Pipeline Tracker

Displays active sales opportunities from qualification and proposal phases to close-won/lost states.

🎯 Lead Details

Inspects specific lead values, estimated close dates, confidence levels, and assigned products.

3.3. Cases

The **Cases Module** is a customer service ticket explorer that logs and tracks customer complaints, requests, and inquiries. It helps ensure SLAs are met and customer issues are resolved in a timely manner.

Figure 3.3: Service cases overview showing ticket list, priorities, and SLA statuses.

🛠️ Support Ticket Management

View ticket details, query category classifications, assignments, and resolution notes.

🚨 Priority & SLA Status

Flags high-priority cases with distinct badges to warn support agents of nearing SLA deadlines.

3.4. Mandates

The **Mandates Module** lists all active standing orders, direct debits, and authorized recurring transaction approvals for the customer. It provides complete transparency over automated bank clearances.

Figure 3.4: Mandates dashboard highlighting authorized payees, payment limits, and frequency configurations.

🔄 Active Clearances

Lists payees, payment intervals, mandate types, and execution status configurations.

🛡️ Audit Control

Displays starting and ending dates, transaction limits, and mandate reference numbers.

3.5. Credit Cards

The **Cards Module** details all linked credit cards, limits, balances, payment dates, and reward points. You can select individual cards to inspect transaction parameters, block statuses, and design visuals.

Figure 3.5: Credit Cards panel displaying active cards, credit limit utilization, and design aesthetics.

💳 Digital Card Designs

Displays a digital card mockup showing design presets matching the customer's active card tier.

📊 Real-Time Limits

Inspects total credit limits, available balances, payment status, and interest cycles.

4. Interactive Onboarding Tour

For new users, the application includes a built-in step-by-step **Interactive Tour**. It highlights key components of the UI and introduces the controls.

1

Trigger Onboarding

Click the hamburger button (⋮) in the top-right header, then select **Start Tour**.

2

Follow Steps

The dashboard will dim, and a glowing highlight capsule will guide you through the client info sidebar, 3D avatar, and quick access modules sequentially.
